# General Allocation & School Calendar Rules

This document outlines the standard bell times, periods, and daily routine for Al-Ihyaz Academy.

---

## 1. Daily Routine & Period Times

### Monday to Thursday (8 Regular Teaching Periods)
* **07:40 – 08:10**: Assembly (Monday only; school gathering and prayers)
* **08:10 – 08:40**: Period 1 (30 mins opening contact)
* **08:40 – 09:20**: Period 2 (40 mins)
* **09:20 – 10:00**: Period 3 (40 mins)
* **10:00 – 10:40**: Period 4 (40 mins)
* **10:40 – 11:10**: **BREAK FAST** (30 mins communal break)
* **11:10 – 11:50**: Period 5 (40 mins)
* **11:50 – 12:30**: Period 6 (40 mins)
* **12:30 – 01:10**: Period 7 (40 mins)
* **01:10 – 01:50**: Period 8 (40 mins)
* **01:50 – 02:00**: Sweeping & Orderly Dismissal (10 mins)

### Friday (Juma'at Special Schedule: 6 Condensed Periods)
* **07:40 – 08:10**: Assembly
* **08:10 – 08:40**: Period 1 (30 mins)
* **08:40 – 09:10**: Period 2 (30 mins)
* **09:10 – 09:40**: Period 3 (30 mins)
* **09:40 – 10:10**: Period 4 (30 mins)
* **10:10 – 10:40**: **BREAK FAST** (30 mins)
* **10:40 – 11:10**: Period 5 (30 mins)
* **11:10 – 11:40**: Period 6 (30 mins)
* **11:40 – 11:50**: Sweeping & Final Inspection
* **11:50**: School closes for Juma'at Prayers and Weekend Dismissal.
* **Periods 7 & 8 are strictly closed on Friday.**

---

## 2. Total Weekly Contact Capacity

* Monday to Thursday: 8 periods/day × 4 days = 32 periods
* Friday: 6 periods/day = 6 periods
* **Total available periods per week per class = 38 periods.**
